Who We Are

We’re a group of plant lovers, garden enthusiasts, and eco-advocates dedicated to spreading awareness of the magic spring brings. From tulips to herbs, we celebrate the healing and happiness that flowers and plants offer each year.

Started in 2023 in a small greenhouse in Oregon, we’ve now grown into a community of thousands sharing the same love for spring blooms.

Our Mission: To educate, inspire, and provide access to spring flowers and plants while promoting sustainable gardening practices.
