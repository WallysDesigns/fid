Garlic Salmon

This is a simple and delicious recipe for garlic salmon.

Ingredients

  • 4 salmon fillets
  • 2 tablespoons olive oil
  • 4 cloves garlic, minced
  • Salt and pepper to taste
  • 1 lemon, sliced

Instructions

  1. Preheat your oven to 375°F (190°C).
  2. Place the salmon fillets on a baking sheet lined with parchment paper.
  3. Drizzle the olive oil over the salmon fillets.
  4. Sprinkle the minced garlic evenly over the salmon.
  5. Season with salt and pepper to taste.
  6. Place lemon slices on top of the salmon fillets.
  7. Bake in the preheated oven for 15-20 minutes, or until the salmon is cooked through and flakes easily with a fork.

Enjoy your delicious garlic salmon!
