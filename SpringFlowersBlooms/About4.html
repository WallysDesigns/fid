Who We Are

Spring Bloom started in 2023 with a small team of gardeners and nature lovers in Oregon. Our mission is to make spring gardening fun, accessible, and sustainable for everyone.

We provide education, fresh flowers, eco-friendly gardening tools, and host events to celebrate nature’s beauty. Join our community and be part of the bloom revolution!

  • Eco-friendly gardening tips
  • Monthly flower deliveries
  • Workshops and plant swaps
